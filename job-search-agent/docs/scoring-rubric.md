# 5-dimension scoring rubric

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Function | 30 | Is the day-job the candidate's actual craft? |
| Domain | 20 | Industry/sector adjacency |
| Seniority | 20 | Band fit both ways - overqualification is a fail mode too |
| Keywords | 20 | JD language coverable truthfully from the bank |
| AI signal | 10 | Does the role reward demonstrated AI shipping? |

**Gate:** 85% for cold applications; softened for recruiter- or referral-routed roles.
**Output contract:** verdict + per-dimension score + gaps (bullets) + bridges (actions to reach 80%+) + disclosure list.
